# Copy APP v0.1.1: iOS 16 and 17 device validation

[English](v0.1.1.md) · [简体中文](v0.1.1.zh-CN.md)

This historical checkpoint builds on the [v0.1.0 two-device record](../releases/v0.1.0.md). The recorded local wheel was named `copyapp-0.1.1-py3-none-any.whl`; it is not a published download. For a current installation, follow the [Mac setup](../../README.md#start-here).

The new XR cases are `ios16-fixture-self-tunnel-install-20261017` and `ios16-fixture-ios-mcp-{counter,notes,catalog}-20261017`. Each passed `copyapp verify`. The matching iOS 17 cases and the older formal paired checks are listed in [v0.1.0](../releases/v0.1.0.md). Raw cases, IPAs, screenshots and device credentials remain local under ignored `cases/` and are not published.

`copyapp install` now creates or reuses a temporary USB SSH forward for its exact `--udid`, verifies the phone identity through the pinned host key, and closes a forward it created after the operation. Separate SSH host-key files and local ports are still required when both phones are connected. This removes the manual forward step previously required for direct TrollStore installation.

Release checks: 309 local unit tests passed; the v0.1.1 wheel metadata and CLI entry point were inspected. The local wheel SHA-256 is `e5af46b9385a6fa1596c16c65a870bceb814a043c4a8904c5af8ade46e232336`.

Support means the specified devices passed these **selected-flow** gates. The fixture covers SwiftUI/UserDefaults, UIKit/SQLite, a framework and a packaged share extension; the extension's user-facing invocation and SQLite row contents were not checked in this release. Calculator and encrypted third-party App pilots provide different-stack evidence, but neither a fresh zero-shot clone of an unfamiliar App nor whole-App equivalence has been established. The iOS 26 phone remains untested. RootHide uses mobile SSH for the verified encrypted-package route; its installed Frida 16.1.4 and root `root-run` paths remain unsupported in the tested setup. See [the iPhone 14 record](../relaxin-roothide-iphone14.md).
