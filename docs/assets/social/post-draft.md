# Copy APP X announcement

Published as [one X post with four images](https://x.com/everettjf/status/2111909890046501003) on 2026-10-18.

These four 1600 × 900 cards use a light background and fine grid, with green from the [project website](https://xnu.app/copyapp/) as the main accent. Orange, purple and pink distinguish secondary content. The captured Calculator screenshots are only resized and placed inside the comparison card; their visible content is not retouched. The Hopper card is an illustration of a recorded excerpt, not a screenshot or the original source code.

## X post

The four images below accompany one post:

> Copy APP is open source. It uses a jailbroken iPhone to collect package, sandbox, UI, Frida and selected native-code evidence for a cited report, then helps build an independent iOS app and compare selected flows on the same device.
>
> Real-device tests:
> • iPhone XR · iOS 16.1.1 · Dopamine — SSH, Frida, UI and selected rebuilds
> • iPhone 14 · iOS 17.3.1 · Relaxin/RootHide — Calculator analysis and paired UI checks
> • iPhone 11 · iOS 26.0.1 · Relaxin/RootHide — Calculator and Clock checks
>
> These are scoped results; current gaps are documented in the repo.
> https://github.com/everettjf/copyapp

## Image order and alt text

1. **[System map](01-system-map.png)** — Light, grid-backed diagram showing a Mac running the coding agent, Copy APP CLI, evidence store and Xcode, connected by USB to a jailbroken iPhone that supplies OpenSSH files, UI observations and Frida traces.
2. **[Evidence](02-evidence.png)** — Green, purple, orange and pink package, sandbox, UI and runtime evidence cards beside a designed excerpt of a recorded iOS 17 Calculator Hopper function. The excerpt does not claim to be original source code or the complete algorithm.
3. **[Calculator comparison](03-calculator-comparison.png)** — Original and independent Calculator screenshots from the same iPhone 14 on iOS 17.3.1 after a selected 7 + 8 = 15 flow. The screenshots are resized without retouching inside green and pink comparison panels; remaining differences are documented.
4. **[Deliverables](04-deliverables.png)** — Light green and orange panels: a cited analysis report and an independent runnable iOS app, followed by same-device comparison and explicit coverage gaps. The tested paths shown are iOS 16, 17 and 26 on named devices.

The illustrations are generated by [generate_cards.py](generate_cards.py) from text and the public, selected screenshots in `docs/assets/`. Raw evidence cases remain local.
