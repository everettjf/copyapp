# Copy APP v0.1.17: first selected-flow milestone

[English](v0.1.17.md) · [简体中文](v0.1.17.zh-CN.md)

This patch release marks the first verified **selected-flow** milestone on jailbroken iOS 16 and iOS 17 devices. Copy APP can collect source-linked App evidence, produce a reviewed analysis report, guide an independent runnable iOS rebuild, and bind same-device behavior and visual checks to a delivery case. The [iPhone XR / iOS 16.1.1 Notes study](../notes-xr-workflow.md) and [iPhone 14 / iOS 17.3.1 Calculator work](../ios17-calculator-fresh-from-report.md) show the tested scope. They do not establish arbitrary-App autonomy or complete feature and visual parity.

The new `coverage-plan` command freezes a reviewed inventory of known user-visible capabilities, importance weights, selected flows, deferred/blocked items and unknown areas. Plans can be revised without overwriting the previous target. `coverage-delivery` binds one or more verified real-device `agent-delivery` cases and explicit per-capability review decisions. It reports the target percentage, completion of the selected milestone and verified coverage of the known inventory separately. A capability cannot be marked verified when its mapped flow lacks a passing behavior assertion or completed visual review without a major difference. The [method and CLI examples](../coverage-targets.md) explain the denominator and review limits.

QA: **324 Python tests passed**. Both new coverage case types passed `copyapp verify` in a local offline exercise using the saved XR Notes report, rebuild plan and real-device delivery. Its five-item inventory is a bounded demonstration, not a whole-Notes coverage estimate. The source-linked iOS 16/17 cases remain the milestone's device evidence; this patch does not claim a fresh device run for every supported path.

**Next stage:** iOS 26 device setup and evidence-backed validation. iOS 26 is untested in this release. General unfamiliar-App discovery and new-source generation, account/server behavior, and full App equivalence remain open.
